---
description: Wrap up a roadmap step
argument-hint: <step name>
---

I think I have finished this roadmap step: $ARGUMENTS

1. Find the step in `docs/roadmap.md` and check its "Done when" line against
   the repository. Tell me honestly whether it is met, and what is missing if
   not.
2. Quiz me with one short question per concept listed for the step, one at a
   time. Give feedback on each answer, and tell me which concepts I should
   revisit before ticking them off in the build map.
3. Draft dev log entries for this step (Progress, plus Learned, Problem or
   Decision where they apply) and ask before adding them to `docs/dev-log.md`.
4. If a significant decision was made, suggest an ADR.
5. Remind me to mark the step done and tick the concepts in the build map.
